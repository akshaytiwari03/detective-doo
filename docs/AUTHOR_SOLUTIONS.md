experience and is not linked from the running site. Do not share it with players.

## 01 — The Glasshouse at Hollin Court
- Culprit: Thomas Pell, head gardener.
- Motive: to avoid exposure and dismissal over selling the Hollin Court orchid divisions.
- Method: fresh monkshood steeped into the whole cordial bottle in the potting room before 10:30 p.m.
- Decisive clues: EV-02 (boiler log in his hand, 11:15 not 10:00), MSG-02 (journal: cut the monkshood), EV-01 (whole-bottle poisoning after 7 p.m.), EV-05 (missing divisions he alone could make).

## 02 — Dead Air
- Culprit: Roy Tanner, sales manager.
- Motive: to stop Lou's air-log exposing years of billing fraud at Monday's meeting.
- Method: struck twice with the Studio A microphone base; scene staged as a fall; automation started to cover the silence.
- Decisive clues: EV-01 (his own alarm code 2:24/2:52), EV-02 (dead air not Lou's doing), EV-05 (base is the weapon; his cut palm), MSG-01 (pocket-dial admission he was there), EV-03 (the fraud).

## 03 — The Dome at Cerro Alto
- Culprit: Dr Felix Marr, visiting astronomer.
- Motive: to prevent publication of proof his famous nova was taken from Brandt's 1958 plates.
- Method: forced from the catwalk; dome then rotated 40° so the fall seemed to come from the fixed stair.
- Decisive clues: EV-01 (dome moved by someone who'd never used the box, while others were in the kitchen), EV-02 (he replaced the log page she wrote on), EV-03 (dark darkroom / dry plate 7), CS-02 (catwalk vs. stair), MSG-01 ("priority matter closed").

## 04 — Slack Water at Cardew Light
- Culprit: Jenna Trevail, business partner / sister-in-law.
- Motive: to gain the whole lease (Clause 14 survivorship) and force through the blocked sale.
- Method: crossed in the mainland tender, struck her on the ramp with the blue boat hook, dragged her into the slipway pool.
- Decisive clues: EV-02 (her phone on the island Wi-Fi 22:48–23:21), EV-03 (wet drysuit + slipway shell sand), EV-01 (only the tender moved), EV-04 (Clause 14 / £1.4m option), CS-02 (the blue boathouse hook).

## 05 — The Understudy's Cue
- Culprit: Harold Benning, stage manager.
- Motive: to stop Vivienne exposing his sale of house seats, with a bookmaker collecting the debt.
- Method: poison dropped into the stoppered prop decanter on his props table during the ASM's absence at the stage door.
- Decisive clues: EV-01 (eight minutes, one man), EV-04 (burned gloves + chemist's paper in the furnace), MSG-01 (bookmaker's threat), EV-02 (poison added after stoppering), CS-04 (he never went to the stage door).

## 06 — The Last Reel
- Culprit: Vikram Rao, chief editor.
- Motive: to keep authorship/credit of the final reel from being handed to a younger editor.
- Method: stabbed with the bench splicer already in hand cutting the reel; door bolted from the corridor with splicing tape ("Rao's lock"); running Moviola faked a living occupant.
- Decisive clues: EV-02 (the film's clock: reel ran 1:12, stalled 1:33 with blood on it — death before his 1:40 "alive"), EV-05 (slate frames he cut), EV-01 (the corridor-bolt trick he invented), MSG-01 (the Mehta contract), CS-04 (his false log page).

## 07 — The Reykjavík Gambit
- Culprit: Stefán Ólafsson, chief arbiter.
- Motive: to bury the €40,000 bribe (disabling the anti-cheating screening) Vasiliev was about to expose that evening.
- Method: soluble cardiac drug dropped into Vasiliev's individual glass at the trolley during the steward's absence; next refill dissolved it.
- Decisive clues: EV-01 (only he approached the glass off-rhythm with hands hidden), EV-02 (one glass, not the carafe), EV-03 (dossier names him + bank reference), MSG-01 ("there cannot be a Sunday presentation"), CS-05 (him at the trolley during the steward's absence).

## 08 — Vintage of the Drowned Vine
- Culprit: Dale Prosser, chief winemaker.
- Motive: eighteen years unacknowledged plus the own-label wine killed by the sale of the Drei Brüder block.
- Method: struck at the catwalk and put into the CO2-laden Fermenter 3; gantry used to submerge the body; handwritten tank book falsified.
- Decisive clues: EV-01 (three logs: probe dip 23:40 + load-cell lift 23:58 vs. his quiet book), EV-05 (only he could/did falsify the book), CS-03 (the gantry lift needs a cellar operator), MSG-01 ("don't sulk" note), CS-02 (pre-immersion blow).

### Fair-play check (summary)
For every case: at least three independent decisive clues (capability/access, timing/opportunity, and a contradiction or document); every clue is reachable before the accusation and selectable in the accusation form; every alternate suspect has an explicit innocent explanation; red herrings resolve to real secrets, not random misdirection; culprit list-positions vary across cases.
